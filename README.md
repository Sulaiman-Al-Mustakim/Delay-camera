# Delay Camera

**Does your laptop camera app have no timer for videos? Use this.**

Press Record, get a countdown (5 seconds), and recording starts.

## Quick start

1. Download `index.html`.
2. Double click it.
3. Click **Allow** for camera and microphone.
4. Press **Record**. Press **Stop**. Press **Download**.

Free. Nothing is uploaded. Your video stays on your computer.
