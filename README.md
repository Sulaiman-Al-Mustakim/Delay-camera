# Delay Camera

Record a video from your camera with a countdown first.

Press Record. Wait 5 seconds. Recording starts. That is all.

It is free. It runs in your browser. **Nothing is uploaded.** Your video stays on your computer.

## What it can do

- Countdown before recording (3, 5, 10 or 15 seconds, or none)
- Pause and Resume
- Full screen
- Saves as MP4 (in Chrome and Edge)
- Watch your video first, then download it
- Pick your camera and microphone
- Microphone level bar, so you know your voice is heard

## How to use it

1. Open the page.
2. Click **Allow** when the browser asks for camera and microphone.
3. Press **Record**. Wait for the countdown.
4. Press **Stop** when you finish.
5. Watch your video. Press **Download** if you like it.

## Keyboard keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| F | Full screen on or off |
| P | Pause or Resume |
| Esc | Cancel the countdown |

## Use it on your own laptop (easiest way)

1. Download the file `index.html`.
2. Double click it.
3. It opens in your browser. Done.

## Put it on the internet with GitHub Pages (free)

You do not need to type any commands.

1. Go to github.com and sign in.
2. Click **New repository**. Name it `delay-camera`. Keep it **Public**. Click **Create repository**.
3. Click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Drag these 3 files in: `index.html`, `README.md`, `LICENSE`.
5. Click **Commit changes**.
6. Click **Settings**, then **Pages** (left side).
7. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`. Click **Save**.
8. Wait 1 or 2 minutes. Refresh the page.
9. Your link will show at the top. It looks like this:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/delay-camera/`

The camera only works on `https://` links, files on your computer, or `localhost`. GitHub Pages gives you `https://`, so it works.

## Got a WEBM file but need MP4?

Use one of these free tools:

- **Clipchamp** (already in Windows): add the video, press Export.
- **VLC**: Media, then Convert / Save.

## Problems and fixes

**The camera does not start.**
Click the lock icon next to the web address. Allow camera and microphone. Press Try again.

**It says the camera is busy.**
Close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, Teams, Camera). Press Try again.

**No sound in my video.**
Open Settings. Choose the right microphone. Speak. The green bar should move.

**I see my face flipped.**
That is only the preview. The saved video is not flipped. You can turn the mirror off in Settings.

## Privacy

This page has no server. It sends nothing anywhere. It only remembers your Settings in your own browser.
